Sofer Asirit Unicode

Original font: x-asirt0.TTF, supplied by Yehuda Weisz on 6 October 2026.
Original family: X-asirit5 tagim.
Original copyright: Copyright 1994, hafozas oas.

This conversion maps the original symbol slots F0E0-F0FA to Unicode Hebrew
U+05D0-U+05EA. The glyph outlines, widths, and original copyright remain intact.
It does not claim to verify letter forms for writing. Original embedding flags
are retained. The conversion script is tools/convert-asirit.py.
